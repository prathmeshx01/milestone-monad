Built around accountability

Every contribution has a plan.

A simple funding flow with clear expectations from the start.

01 / DEFINE

Set the goal

Campaign creators explain the project, set a funding target, and describe the milestones they intend to complete.

02 / CONTRIBUTE

Back the idea

Supporters can see the campaign goal and progress. In the planned on-chain version, contributions will be recorded by a Monad smart contract.

03 / SHOW PROGRESS

Move milestone by milestone

Creators share evidence of progress. The intended contract rules will govern eligible releases; evidence review itself still requires a defined approval process.
